Scenario 7: Cross-Origin Form Behavior & Limitations

Cross-Origin Form Interception & Boundaries

Demonstrates supported capturing-phase suppression for marked controlled test actions vs. architectural sensor limitations on ambient unmarked forms.

Security Boundary & Platform Constraint Notice:
Browser extensions execute in user-space and cannot universally undo JavaScript handlers or network requests that have already occurred. Guardrail does not claim universal interception or guaranteed rollback. Synchronous pre-execution halting is supported on controlled test targets via capturing-phase listeners; ambient unmarked forms emit advisory warnings without false claims of interception.

1. Supported Controlled Cross-Origin Form (Synchronous Suppression)

Target: https://untrusted-partner.external.net/auth (Marked with data-guardrail-test-action="test-cross-origin-protect").

2. Ambient Unmarked Form (Documented Platform Boundary)

Unmarked form. Guardrail detects cross-origin credentials and emits advisory logs, but does not falsely claim the request was cancelled or held.
